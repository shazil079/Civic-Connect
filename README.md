# Civic-Connect
🏙️ Civic Connect is a citizen-driven platform that helps the public report civic issues such as waste dumping, broken streetlights, potholes, and other community problems. Citizens can upload photos and details of issues, while city councils can track, verify, and resolve reports. 
🏙️ Civic Connect

Civic Connect is a citizen-focused platform designed to bridge the gap between the public and local government authorities.

The platform allows citizens to report public issues such as illegal waste dumping, broken streetlights, potholes, damaged roads, and other civic problems by uploading photos and providing relevant details.

City councils can view these reports, verify the issues, and take appropriate action to resolve them. This creates a more transparent and efficient way for communities to communicate with local authorities.

⭐ Key Features

📸 Report Civic Issues – Citizens can report problems with photos and descriptions.

🏛️ Government Dashboard – City councils can view, manage, and track reported issues.

📍 Issue Tracking – Reports can be monitored from submission through resolution.

✅ Report Verification – Helps identify genuine and useful reports.

🏆 Points & Leaderboard – Users earn points for submitting genuine reports and can compete on a community leaderboard.

🌍 Community Participation – Encourages citizens to actively contribute to improving their city.

🔔 Transparent Resolution – Helps citizens stay informed about the status of reported issues.

🎯 Goal

The goal of Civic Connect is to make civic issue reporting easier, faster, and more transparent while encouraging citizens to actively participate in improving their communities.

Civic Connect — Report. Participate. Improve your city.
